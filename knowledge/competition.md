COMPETITION RULES — VOLUME (as applied in the current campus rounds; confirm your school's Round Notice):
Q: What happens if I do not meet the $7,000 weekly trading volume?
Aim for around $7,000 in total trading volume each week. A small shortfall can be considered, but being significantly below the requirement may mean you do not qualify for that week. You can try again the following week. Support can review your specific result; there is no published numerical tolerance.
Q: Am I disqualified if I do not meet the minimum volume?
Missing the weekly volume target does not automatically eliminate you from the competition. Weekly qualification and elimination are separate. The overall loss rule eliminates an account from the round if it loses 35% or more of its original competition balance, subject to AndX verification.
Q: Do buying and selling both count toward trading volume?
Yes. Each executed buy and sell counts separately at its full executed value: buying $100 and later selling $100 contributes $200.
Q: Does derivatives volume use my margin or the full position size?
The full executed notional value. $100 of margin on a $1,000 position counts as $1,000 when opened and another $1,000 when closed.
Q: Do pending, cancelled or partially filled orders count?
Only executed trades count. An unfilled order adds nothing; a cancelled order contributes only the portion executed before cancellation; a partial fill contributes its filled portion.
Q: Does extra volume carry over to the following week?
Volume is assessed per seven-day trading period; do not assume it carries over. Support can confirm period boundaries.
Q: What time and timezone does each day and week start and end?
The daily loss-limit reset is 00:00 UTC. Volume is assessed over seven-day periods; ask support for your school's exact weekly boundaries and start/end dates.
Q: How can I check my trading volume?
Go to platform.andx.ai/trading-competition, select your school and open the leaderboard. If your recorded volume looks wrong, contact support with your account details and executed trades.
Q: Will I be penalized for missing volume because of a technical issue?
Verified platform issues can be reviewed so you are not unfairly penalized for the volume they prevented. Report promptly with screenshots and affected times (the team previously confirmed a volume exception for an XRP issue). Support must review how an issue affected your own account.
Q: Is the volume requirement about pushing volume?
No — the official rules say rankings reward performance; minimum-activity rules are about genuine participation. Placing unnecessary trades does not help your score, and wash trading, self-matching or artificial volume means disqualification.

COMPETITION RULES — ROI AND LOSS LIMITS:
Q: How is ROI calculated?
ROI = (current equity − starting equity − net deposits) ÷ starting equity (× 100 for a percentage). Starting equity is your portfolio value at the close of day zero. Net deposits = deposits minus withdrawals, so moving money in or out never counts as trading profit. Trading fees are reimbursed under the competition and added back, so they do not count against performance. Open positions are valued at the current AndX market price when the leaderboard is calculated. The official rules add that no fee refund may be counted twice and that final results come from verified records.
Q: How does the 15% daily loss limit work?
Measured from your account value at 00:00 UTC at the start of the competition day (not from the day's highest balance). If the account loses 15% or more during that day, competition trading is suspended for the rest of that day and resumes at the next daily reset. From a $100 daily start, $85 reaches the limit, subject to the system calculation.
Q: What is the 35% elimination rule?
If a competition account loses 35% or more of its original competition balance, the student is eliminated from that round, subject to final AndX verification ($65 or less from $100).
Q: When does the daily loss limit reset?
At 00:00 UTC — midnight UTC, not your local midnight.
Q: Does the daily loss limit include open positions? Are positions closed automatically?
Open positions are valued at market prices for the leaderboard; the exact risk-engine treatment of unrealized losses should be confirmed with support. A daily breaker stops new trading until the reset; Stop leaves open positions in place; Stop & close all requests market closure. Check your open positions and protective orders on ANDX if a limit triggers. Do not assume every loss-limit event closes positions.
Q: Can I owe money beyond the competition funds?
The competition provides the starting capital; a blanket guarantee about losses beyond that balance has not been confirmed, and derivatives/liquidation terms matter. Ask support to confirm protections before using leverage or adding personal funds.
Q: Which fees are reimbursed, and when?
Competition trading fees have been described as credited back daily, so they should not count against performance. If a credit is missing, contact support with trade dates and fees. Withdrawal fees, liquidation charges or funding payments are not established as covered — ask support about funding payments before holding a derivatives position overnight.
Q: Why does leaderboard ROI differ from the profit shown in my account?
The two views may cover different periods or treat deposits, open positions and fee credits differently. Competition ROI is measured against competition starting equity. Send both screenshots to support if the difference is unexplained.
Q: Ties and advancement?
After the volume requirement, ranking is by ROI; the deck says top 3 per school by ROI plus 40 wildcards advance. The official rules break ties by higher realized dollar profit, then lower maximum drawdown, then the earlier time the final ROI was reached, unless a round publishes a different tie-breaker. Support confirms the number advancing and any wildcard rules for your school.
Q: What happens if nobody qualifies or everyone has negative ROI? Does my balance reset when I advance?
The team has discussed an extra fresh-start week (new ROI period, no volume requirement) — support must confirm whether it is activated for your school, its dates and how many advance. A fresh ROI period does not automatically replenish funds or close positions; wait for your round instructions.
Q: Can I trade manually? Can I run multiple bots or my own bot?
This is a bot competition; manual trades count only when the Round Notice expressly authorizes them. You may use a pre-built bot or your own strategy (Custom). Running several bots on one account has not been confirmed — ask support first.

WHY THE BOT IS OR ISN'T TRADING:
Q: My bot is active / connected but not making trades.
Checklist in order: (1) status says running / LIVE? (2) Save & test shows no red error? (3) Derivatives toggled ON (if your bot trades derivatives)? (4) read the Activity log. If all green, the bot is being selective on purpose: it skips trades when spreads are too wide, when the bigger trend disagrees, after two stop-outs (cooldown), and it spaces entries out (roughly one per hour per pair). Quiet markets = quiet bot; a typical day is a handful of trades. There is no daily trade cap any more. If there is an error, quote it and use the matching fix; if no error but the inactivity seems unusual, send support the bot status, last trade time and last five Activity log lines.
Q: How can I confirm my bot is running and participating? Dashboard status + activity/trade history at bot.andx.ai, then your school leaderboard. A running bot does not by itself prove enrollment.
Q: How can I confirm my trades are counted? Executed trades in platform.andx.ai/spot or platform.andx.ai/derivatives order history, compared with your competition volume. A submitted order is not an executed trade. Report discrepancies with order IDs and timestamps.
Q: Do I need to leave my laptop on? No — the hosted bot runs on ANDX servers 24/7 after Start. A downloaded laptop bot stops when the computer sleeps; stop the older bot before switching so you do not run duplicates.
Q: Why did my bot short? Why only ~10 coins? Derivatives mode trades long and short; bots trade only pairs with real liquidity.
Q: What are the default risk settings? About 1% of the account risked per trade, stop-losses about 3×ATR away placed on the exchange, a cap on open positions and capped leverage. Stops do not guarantee an exact exit price during gaps or slippage.
Q: Why is my bot losing money? Trading is hard and most short windows are negative — that is real markets, not a glitch. Nobody should expect guaranteed profit. What you should NOT see is a single loss much bigger than about 1% of your account; report that.
Q: How do I stop the bot? Stop halts new trading and leaves positions open (exchange-side stops stay); Stop & close all requests market closure of all positions — verify on ANDX. Updates wait until the bot has no open positions; a bot you stopped stays stopped after restarts.
Q: Balance differs from the exchange? Refresh both pages; the bot shows the value your ANDX page computes. Report a mismatch lasting more than five minutes. "Balance" is the ANDX account value; "equity" adds open-position profit or loss.
Q: Insufficient funds with a balance? The total may include other assets, another wallet or amounts reserved for open orders; check the available balance for the asset and market; send support screenshots if it still does not match.
Q: XRP shows but cannot be sold / assets missing from the bot? The remaining quantity must meet the minimum order size; a holding and an available-to-sell balance can differ; an existing order may reserve it. Compare exchange and bot views; send screenshots to support — a previous XRP issue needed the technical team.
