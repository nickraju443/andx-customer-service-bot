COMPETITION RULES — VOLUME:
Q: What happens if I do not meet the $7,000 weekly trading volume?
Aim for around $7,000 in total trading volume each week. A small shortfall can be considered, but being significantly below the requirement may mean you do not qualify for that week. You can try again the following week. Support can review your specific result; there is no published numerical tolerance for a shortfall.
Q: Am I disqualified if I do not meet the minimum volume?
Missing the weekly volume target does not automatically eliminate you from the entire competition. Weekly qualification and elimination are separate. The overall loss rule eliminates an account from the round if it loses 35% or more of its original competition balance, subject to AndX verification.
Q: Do buying and selling both count toward trading volume?
Yes. Each executed buy and sell counts separately at its full executed value. Buying $100 and later selling $100 contributes $200 in volume.
Q: Does derivatives volume use my margin or the full position size?
The full executed notional value, not just your margin. $100 of margin used for a $1,000 position counts as $1,000 when opened; closing that $1,000 position adds another $1,000. Actual executed values apply if the price or position size changes.
Q: Do pending, cancelled or partially filled orders count?
Volume is based on executed trades. An unfilled pending order adds nothing. A cancelled order contributes only the portion executed before cancellation; a partially filled order contributes its filled portion. Ask support to review any mismatch with your executed order history.
Q: Is $7,000 a strict cutoff?
The guidance is around $7,000 in weekly trading volume. A small shortfall may be considered, but being significantly below may not qualify for that week. No exact dollar or percentage tolerance has been stated, so support reviews borderline cases.
Q: Does extra volume carry over to the following week?
Volume is assessed for the relevant seven-day trading period. Do not assume extra volume carries over. Support can confirm period boundaries and any special adjustment.
Q: What time and timezone does each day and week start and end?
The daily loss-limit reset is 00:00 UTC. Volume is assessed over seven-day trading periods. Ask support for the exact weekly boundaries and start/end dates for your school; schools may have different schedules.
Q: How can I check my trading volume?
Go to platform.andx.global/trading-competition, select your school and open the leaderboard. Check the volume for the relevant competition period. If your recorded volume looks wrong, contact support with your account details and executed trades.
Q: Will I be penalized for missing volume because of a technical issue?
Verified platform issues can be reviewed so you are not unfairly penalized for the volume they prevented. Report the issue promptly with screenshots and the affected times. The team previously confirmed a volume exception for an XRP issue; support must review how an issue affected your own account.

COMPETITION RULES — ROI AND LOSS LIMITS:
Q: How is ROI calculated?
ROI = (current equity − starting equity − net deposits) ÷ starting equity, multiplied by 100 for a percentage. Starting equity is your portfolio value at the close of day zero. Net deposits means deposits less withdrawals, so moving money in or out is not counted as trading profit. Trading fees are reimbursed under the competition and added back, so they do not count against performance. Open positions are valued at the current AndX market price when the leaderboard is calculated.
Q: How does the 15% daily loss limit work?
The limit is measured from your account value at 00:00 UTC at the start of the competition day (not from the highest balance reached later that day). If your account loses 15% or more during that day, competition trading is suspended for the remainder of that day and resumes at the next eligible daily reset. Example: a $100 daily starting value reaches a 15% loss at $85, subject to the system calculation.
Q: What is the 35% elimination rule?
If a competition account loses 35% or more of its original competition balance, the student is eliminated from that competition round, subject to final AndX verification. Starting from $100, an account value of $65 or less triggers elimination.
Q: When does the daily loss limit reset?
At 00:00 UTC (midnight UTC, not your local midnight). Trading can resume after the next reset if the account has not reached the overall elimination limit or another restriction.
Q: Does the daily loss limit include open positions?
Open positions are valued at current AndX market prices for leaderboard calculations. The daily loss rule uses account value, but the exact risk-engine treatment of unrealized losses should be confirmed with support. Do not assume leaving a losing position open prevents a loss-limit trigger.
Q: Are my positions automatically closed when I hit a loss limit?
A daily breaker stops new trading until its reset. Stop alone leaves open positions in place; Stop & close all requests market closure. Check open positions and their protective orders directly on AndX if a loss limit is triggered. It has not been established that every loss-limit event automatically closes positions.
Q: Can I owe money beyond the competition funds?
The competition provides starting capital, but a blanket guarantee about losses beyond that balance has not been confirmed. Derivatives account and liquidation terms matter. Ask support to confirm the applicable protections before using leverage or adding personal funds.
Q: Which fees are reimbursed, and when?
Competition trading fees have been described as credited back daily, so reimbursed fees should not count against your competition performance. If a credit is missing, contact support with the trade dates and fees. This does not establish that withdrawal fees, liquidation charges or funding payments are covered.
Q: Are derivatives funding payments included in reimbursement?
Funding payments are different from trading fees. Earlier replies described derivatives as having no overnight or funding charges, but that is not confirmed for every contract. Ask support to confirm the exact contract terms and whether any funding payment is reimbursed before holding a position.
Q: How do deposits, withdrawals and fee credits affect ROI?
The ROI formula subtracts net deposits so moving money is not counted as profit, and reimbursed trading fees should not reduce performance. Support should review the timing of credits and any score discrepancy; do not add a fee back again if it is already included in your credited equity.
Q: Why does leaderboard ROI differ from the profit shown in my account?
The two views may cover different time periods or treat deposits, open positions and fee credits differently. Competition ROI is measured against competition starting equity, while an account screen may show another profit measure. Send both screenshots to support if the difference is unexplained.

THE ANDX TRADING BOT — GETTING STARTED:
Q: What is the ANDX trading bot?
An automated trading bot that trades crypto derivatives (long and short) on the ANDX exchange using your own account and API key. You pick a strategy style, press Start, and it scans the markets around the clock — even with your laptop closed if you use the cloud version.
Q: How do I get started?
1. Sign up at bot.andx.ai/university and pick your bot style. 2. Create an API key on your ANDX account (see the checklist). 3. Go to bot.andx.ai and log in with your key — your bot is created automatically. 4. Press Start.
Q: Cloud bot vs downloaded bot?
The cloud bot runs on AndX servers 24/7 — laptop closed, it keeps trading. The downloaded bot runs on your own computer and stops when it sleeps (its "Keep bot running" script helps). The cloud bot calls ANDX from 136.108.104.209; a laptop bot calls from your home IP — the key's IP whitelist must include whichever one you are actually using (both is fine, max 3 IPs). Stop the older bot before switching so you do not run duplicate instances.
Q: Where do I put my API key?
On your bot dashboard, scroll to the API keys box, paste the key and secret, press "Save & test keys". If it says saved/valid, press Start. Never share credentials in chat or screenshots.
Q: How much money do I need?
Competition accounts start with $100. The bot sizes trades as a percentage of your account, but exchange minimum order sizes still apply — very small accounts cannot trade the most expensive coins (see the BTC error below).
Q: What is the "trading budget" field?
An optional cap on how much of your account the bot trades with. Leave it blank and the bot uses the whole account. Enter a number (like 50) and the bot sizes all trades as if that is all it has.
Q: What are the three bot styles?
Balanced (violet): reads the market and switches between trend-riding and dip-buying — the all-weather default. Trend (orange): turtle-style breakout hunter — buys strength breaking out, exits when the move stalls; lively in moving markets, quiet in chop. Custom (teal): a classic RSI dip-buyer meant as a starting point — open the strategy editor and make it yours. Pick one with the buttons at the top of the dashboard; it applies instantly. If you chose one at signup, your bot starts on it automatically.
Q: Can I download my bot / get the code?
Yes — the "Download my bot" button on the dashboard saves your current strategy code as a Python file. There is also a full runnable bot you can install on your own computer.
Q: Can I change bots or strategies during the competition?
Yes — the Balanced, Trend or Custom style buttons, the symbol and risk settings, or the strategy editor. Style changes apply immediately. Check existing positions before changing settings. If an edit breaks the strategy, use "Restore original" and reapply the edit carefully.
Q: I signed up but changed my mind about my bot style.
The three style buttons on your dashboard switch you any time, in one click.

Q: Why is an active bot not showing trading activity?
Check the bot activity log and exchange order history. If there are no executions, review errors, available funds, strategy conditions and loss-limit status. If executions exist but the leaderboard has not reflected them, send support the order IDs and screenshots.
Q: How long should I wait before reporting that the bot is not trading?
There is no universal waiting period because strategies trade under different conditions. Report an error immediately. If there is no error but the inactivity seems unusual, send support your bot status, last trade time and activity log.
Q: Is there a daily limit on trades?
No. There used to be a daily cap; it was removed. Entries are spaced out hourly so the bot does not spam, and exits are always allowed. You can set your own cap in settings.
Q: Why does my bot only trade about 10 coins when ANDX lists more?
It trades the pairs with real liquidity on derivatives. Thin pairs have wide spreads that eat your account on every round trip — scanning them adds risk, not profit.
Q: Why did my bot SHORT something?
Derivatives mode trades both directions — long when signals point up, short when they point down. That is normal and intended.
Q: What are the default risk settings?
About 1% of your account risked per trade, stop-losses about 3×ATR away, a cap on how many positions can be open at once, and capped leverage. The bot uses exchange-side stop-loss and take-profit orders — confirm they were accepted for each position. Stops do not guarantee an exact exit price during gaps, slippage or execution failures.
Q: Why is my bot losing money?
Honest answer: trading is hard and most short windows are negative — that is real markets, not a glitch. The bot takes many small controlled losses (the 1% risk working) and needs winners to outrun them, which does not happen every day. Nobody should expect guaranteed profit — this is a learning competition with real market consequences. What you should NOT see: single losses much bigger than about 1% of your account. If you do, report it.
Q: Can the bot lose more than what is in my account?
The risk target is about 1% per trade with leverage limits and exchange-side stops, but these controls do not establish that losses cannot exceed the balance. Ask support to confirm the applicable account and liquidation protections. Withdrawal permissions must stay disabled.
Q: Do I need to leave my laptop on or the browser open?
No. The hosted bot at bot.andx.ai runs on AndX servers after you press Start My Bot and confirm. Check in periodically to review status and trades. The downloaded laptop bot does stop when your computer sleeps.
Q: Will the bot continue if I lose internet or close the app?
Yes for the hosted bot — closing your app or losing your own connection does not stop it. Its operation still depends on its connection to AndX, valid API access and account trading restrictions.
Q: Do I need to press Start again after an update or server restart?
A previously running bot resumes after a restart or update; a bot you stopped stays stopped. Check the status afterward; if it did not recover, use the startup troubleshooting steps.
Q: Updates got pushed — will that interrupt my open trades?
Updates wait until the bot has no open positions before applying. After any interruption, verify bot status and positions rather than assuming nothing changed.
Q: How do I stop the bot, and what happens to my positions?
Stop halts new trading and leaves existing positions open; accepted stop-loss and take-profit orders remain on the exchange. Stop & close all requests market closure of all open positions. Verify fills and remaining positions on AndX — do not assume every protective order was placed or every close request filled.
Q: My equity chart jumped around / showed a weird spike.
An earlier chart issue was fixed and account values now come from AndX. If you still see a sudden spike that your ANDX page does not show, report it.

BALANCES, WALLETS AND ASSETS:

Q: What is the difference between balance and equity on my dashboard?
Balance is the AndX account value; equity is balance plus open-position profit or loss. Use the displayed labels when reporting a mismatch.
Q: Which wallet should hold funds for the bot?
Funds must be available in the wallet used by the bot for the selected trading market. A balance in the main wallet may not be available in a separate trading wallet. Ask support to confirm the correct wallet and transfer process before moving competition funds.
Q: Why do I see "insufficient funds" even though I have a balance?
Your total balance may include other assets, funds in a different wallet, or amounts reserved for open orders or positions. Check the available balance for the asset and market you are trading. If it still does not match, send support screenshots of the balance, order and wallet screens.
Q: Why can I see XRP holdings but cannot sell them?

A displayed holding and an available-to-sell balance can differ. If XRP still appears missing or unavailable, send support screenshots of the wallet, open orders and sell screen; a previous XRP issue required the technical team to review the account.
Q: Why are certain assets not appearing in my bot?
The bot may only show the assets or wallet available to its trading connection, or there may be a synchronization issue. Compare the asset quantity, wallet and open orders on the exchange with the bot view. If an asset is still missing, send support screenshots of both views.
Q: How often does the leaderboard update?
A guaranteed refresh interval has not been confirmed. Compare executed order history with the relevant leaderboard period and send persistent missing volume or trades to support.

SUPPORT AND EVIDENCE:
Q: What evidence should I send for a technical issue?
Your registered email, school, a short description, screenshots with secrets hidden, the time the problem started and its timezone, and relevant order IDs or error messages. Say whether it prevented trading or affected the displayed balance or volume.
Q: How do I request a review of missing volume, rankings or disqualification?
Email support@andx.ai with your school, registered account, the competition week, and the result you believe is incorrect. Include executed order IDs, screenshots and timestamps. The team reviews the records before confirming a correction or reinstatement.
Q: How do I reach a person if the support bot cannot solve my issue?
Email support@andx.ai, or use your school competition WhatsApp group if one was provided. Include your registered email and a brief description. Never post API secrets or identity documents in a group chat.
Q: What information should I never share with support or the bot?
Never send an API secret, password, one-time login code, wallet seed phrase or private key in chat or email. Do not send a screenshot of the API-key page. Enter credentials only in the official bot connection flow at bot.andx.ai. Support can start with your registered email, school, exact error and the last five Activity log lines, with sensitive information removed.
